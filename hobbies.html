Gaming

Gaming is one of my favorite hobbies. I enjoy learning game systems, testing team combinations, and improving character builds.

Honkai: Star Rail is one of the games I play most often. Tribbie is my favorite character, and optimizing her build has become a project of its own.

Programming

I practice programming through university exercises and small personal projects. Each project helps me understand software structure more clearly.

Game Design

I like planning game mechanics, stories, progression systems, and the relationship between player choices and gameplay consequences.

Learning Technology

I enjoy discovering how websites, applications, operating systems, and networks work.

Creative Writing

Writing ideas for characters, settings, and game worlds helps me connect technical skills with imagination.

Why These Hobbies Matter

My hobbies are not separate from my studies. Gaming helps me analyze systems, programming teaches me how to build them, and creative work gives those systems meaning.
